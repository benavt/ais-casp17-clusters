Sampling details

DISCO50 seeds minimum-energy representatives of fine groups within the state, then fills remaining positions by energy-weighted sampling without replacement. States require at least 50 members; no padding or per-fine-cluster quota is used. Saved fixed-budget MCMC diagnostics are exploratory and do not establish convergence or biological populations. Methods
Model counts

Each audited SR215 cohort is grouped using intramodel Cα distances. The same cohort is used across all three residue ranges. RPF, MolProbity and ProteinEBM scores are available as plot colors and in the membership CSVs.

Saved cohorts include models from AFSample2, AFSample3, Boltz2 Exact, OpenDDE, BioEMU, BioEMU2, AlphaFlow MD, AlphaFlow PDB, ESMFlow PDB, SPEACH_AF, and AFCluster. Methods are analyzed jointly within each target. Exact counts are listed here. Different target sequences are analyzed separately.

All projections use the same retained, sequence-aligned Cα distance features and the default terminal-variance mask. Clusters preserve disconnected components of the seed-42 UMAP 15-neighbor graph; candidate subdivisions require feature silhouette ≥ 0.15 and agreement ≥ 0.80 with seeds 7 and 123. Cluster colors identify the same memberships in every projection.

Color by AIS Method, saved cluster labels, or available confidence and similarity scores. Optional ProteinEBM model 6 energy colors use only the saved default conditioning/noise level, t=0.10; this is not a trajectory time. The full-construct energies are reused across residue-range and clustering views, rather than recalculated for each mask. Values are in arbitrary units; lower is better within a target. They are not calibrated across targets. BioEMU and BioEMU2 have no pLDDT/pTM scores and remain gray for those metrics. Confidence-ranked ensembles use only methods emitting comparable confidence; all models remain in the saved structural cohort. The representative-selection policy for this analysis is shown with the cluster table.

Bonsai summarizes models sampled from the saved cluster ensembles. Tree-path distances describe structural differences; colors follow the explorer legend. The tree uses the frozen Cα-distance features with all nonzero PCA axes retained and no whitening or variance scaling. Its root is arbitrary; exploratory tree cuts are not population cluster labels.

Cluster populations describe these generated ensembles. They are not equilibrium probabilities or evidence of transition kinetics. Saved selection highlights reuse completed pilot records: rings identify the original selected model IDs, without changing the cohort. Exact categorical and Metropolis–Hastings highlights show all unique visited models, not a 200-model panel. Cluster balance and τ/IQR choose among saved conditions; the full-construct ProteinEBM conditioning level stays t=0.10. Bonsai shows only highlighted models present in its saved subset. This page never runs a sampler.

For assigned structural clusters, selection weights are proportional to exp(−(E − E_min)/τ) divided by cluster size raised to α. HDBSCAN keeps its aggregate energy-weighted noise mass fixed across balance settings. Weighted unique panels contain 200 models sampled without replacement; DISCO chooses the minimum-energy model in each assigned cluster and excludes noise. Metropolis–Hastings uses four chains with 10,000 proposals each and discards the first 1,000 states per chain. Rejected and repeated states are retained for occupancy diagnostics. Rings show membership, not visit frequency; they do not establish convergence, equilibrium populations or structural accuracy. Exact categorical draws provide the finite-pool comparison. The saved pilots have observed mixing limitations, including assigned clusters that were never visited. The active MCMC status reports saved cluster total variation (including unassigned labels) versus matched independent categorical draws, and the number of unvisited assigned clusters. Lower total variation means closer agreement with the declared target label probabilities; these fixed-budget chains remain exploratory.

For the pooled AFSample3 + BioEMU2 cohort, each saved residue range has an independent search for up to three representative-relative states at TM ≥ 0.80, with at least 50 members per supported state. Selection uses the original complete-cohort HDBSCAN prior and saved full-construct ProteinEBM model 6 energies at t=0.10, α=0.5 and τ equal to one cohort energy IQR. MCMC50 and DISCO50 each select 50 distinct models per state. MCMC retains chain 0 at iteration 10,000 under the product-weight subset law. DISCO seeds minimum-energy fine-cluster representatives within the state and fills remaining positions using energy-weighted sampling without replacement, including qualified original noise models. State counts are bounded search results, not biological populations. Bonsai uses the exact method-selected state union. Scores remain independent color annotations.

TM states are saved representative-relative neighborhoods at the threshold and canonical core shown in the state controls. Current residual results use TM ≥ 0.80 and a bounded residual search; legacy results retain their recorded threshold and discovery policy. Neither gives biological-state populations or an all-pairs structural certificate. MCMC50 uses the preselected chain 0 at iteration 10,000 under a fixed-cardinality product-weight subset law. DISCO50 is a separate within-state energy/coverage control with a different selection law. Each contains exactly 50 distinct saved models per state. Current residual results use the saved full-cohort HDBSCAN prior on both displayed Primary and HDBSCAN views; legacy fine-view priors remain in their recorded provenance. All qualified state members is a membership view, not a sampled panel. State-colored rings preserve the chosen base point colors. Short cores, undersized states, incomplete coverage and exploratory mixing diagnostics remain explicit. PDB and PyMOL session downloads retain the full saved structures, aligned on the TM comparison core; display settings never modify those files.

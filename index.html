Methods & provenance

Sampling methods, analysis settings and frozen source identities.

Saved cohorts include models from AFSample2, AFSample3, Boltz2 Exact, OpenDDE, BioEMU, BioEMU2, AlphaFlow MD, AlphaFlow PDB, ESMFlow PDB, SPEACH_AF, and AFCluster. Methods are analyzed jointly within each target. Exact counts appear above. Different target sequences are analyzed separately.

All projections use the same retained, sequence-aligned Cα distance features and the default terminal-variance mask. Clusters preserve disconnected components of the seed-42 UMAP 15-neighbor graph; candidate subdivisions require feature silhouette ≥ 0.15 and agreement ≥ 0.80 with seeds 7 and 123. Cluster colors identify the same memberships in every projection.

Color by AIS Method, saved cluster labels, or available confidence and similarity scores. Optional ProteinEBM model 6 energy colors use only the saved default conditioning/noise level, t=0.10; this is not a trajectory time. The full-construct energies are reused across residue-range and clustering views, rather than recalculated for each mask. Values are in arbitrary units; lower is better within a target. They are not calibrated across targets. BioEMU and BioEMU2 have no pLDDT/pTM scores and remain gray for those metrics. Confidence-ranked ensembles use only methods emitting comparable confidence; all models remain in the saved structural cohort. The representative-selection policy for this analysis is shown with the cluster table.

Cluster populations describe these generated ensembles. They are not equilibrium probabilities or evidence of transition kinetics. Future MCMC sampling requires a separately defined target distribution and validation.

Method source code: AFSample2 · AFSample3 · AFCluster · SPEACH_AF · AlphaFlow MD / PDB and ESMFlow PDB · BioEMU · BioEMU2 · Boltz2 Exact · OpenDDE. Tree analysis: Bonsai (CC BY-NC 4.0). Structural comparisons: US-align; SR215 reference: ColabFold.
